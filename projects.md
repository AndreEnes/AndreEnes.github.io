## List

- **[Automotive Metrics on the Heart of Joy ECU](metrics/)** *(2026 to present)*  
  An embedded metrics service for BMW's in-vehicle data collection platform, validated on a real ECU with hardware-in-the-loop tests.

- **[A Portable Job Runtime for Vehicle ECUs](runtime/)** *(2025 to present)*  
  The C++ core that installs, validates and runs data collection jobs across different vehicle ECUs.

- **[In-Vehicle Data Collection Jobs](jobs/)** *(2024 to 2025)*  
  Sandboxed AssemblyScript and WebAssembly jobs running on production vehicle ECUs, turning raw signals into structured events.

- **[Vehicle Bus Signal SDK](sdk/)** *(2024 to 2025)*  
  A TypeScript code generator that turns CAN and FlexRay signal definitions into typed AssemblyScript decoders for the data collection jobs.

- **[Sensor System for Industrial Predictive Maintenance](sensorSystem/)** *(2022 to 2023)*  
  Wireless vibration, sound and temperature sensors with ML fault detection, for AGVs and machines that were never built with sensors in mind. My master's dissertation.

- **[Beacon-Based 3D Localisation of a Quadcopter Using Extended Kalman Filter](quadcopter/)** *(2023)*  
  Estimating a drone's position from noisy RF beacon signals, in ROS and Webots.

- **[Ancelotti Robot: Real-Time Facial Gesture Animatronic](ancelotti/)** *(2023)*  
  An animatronic head that copies your facial expressions as you make them.

- **[IoT Dashcam Crash Detection System](dashcam/)** *(2023)*  
  A Raspberry Pi dashcam that detects the impact itself and sends the footage over Telegram.

- **[argentina-opt: Machine Learning Optimisation Toolkit](argentina/)** *(2022)*  
  Give it the output you want and it searches for the inputs that get you there.

- **[SCARA Robotic Arm Project](scara/)** *(2022)*  
  Forward and inverse kinematics, the Jacobian, and trajectory control.

- **[SiiuuuTunes](siiiuuutunes/)** *(2022)*  
  A C++ and GTK attempt at the core of Spotify, with Postgres behind it.

- **[Colour-Sorting Palletiser Robotic Arm](palletizer/)** *(2021)*  
  An Atmega328p arm that sorts pieces by colour, with transistors multiplexing the servo pins.

- **[Bonus](bonus/)**  
  A ray tracer, and an instrument that turns a drawn line into sound.
